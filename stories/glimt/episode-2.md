# Glimt, episode 2: Behind the Presses

English draft 1, 2026-10-30, written in English from the Swedish draft 5 (`episod-2.md`, which stays as it is) and the core loop Henric decided on 30/10. Reviewed the same day by the dramaturg, the director and the feasibility review (`data/glimt-granska/2026-10-30-*.md` in generalassistant), and their findings are in. Recorded the same day with Jessica as Vega. Not yet in the app. The text lives in Manus; this file is its mirror.

**What the episode has to do:** Shape walks for Vega, and for the first time she gets somewhere she has never been, thanks to Shape. Afterwards Shape should know three new things without anyone explaining them: Shape can take her somewhere, and a stop when they get there lets her see it; Shape's walk is her cover, so she can go where she isn't allowed as long as Shape keeps going; and inside the works, where nobody stands still, yes is two knocks.

**The arc:** someone finally asks her the question she has waited four years for, and she doesn't want to go alone. Before she answers, she asks Shape to show her what outside is. Then she goes, with Shape: through a door she has only seen closed, a way Shape chooses, to the back of the presses, where everyone walks in time and the song comes whole. She is given something to carry, and she has no word for what Shape is.

**Style:** as episode 1. Close and spoken, one person on a bad phone line, dry when it's at its worst. She describes only what the phone has read: the light, the weather, how Shape moves and what the map has near Shape. Never people, cars or things. The people on her side never speak in their own voices: she tells Shape what they say, one word per beat, the way she heard it. What she says to herself, marked (to herself) or (low), is her normal voice, a little lower, never a whisper.

**The English:** as episode 1, in Jessica's American.

**Liv's words:** eight short passages from Liv's Here are in Vega's mouth or her world, word for word, with only the spelling fixed. They are listed at the end, with where in Here they come from.

**Length:** about eleven minutes on one walk at episode 1's pace. About ten when the map has nothing in scene 2, eleven or twelve with a place, up to fourteen when the bell comes first.

**Walking or running:** scenes 0 to 2 go as well running. From the door in scene 3 she asks a runner to walk, because knocks and the path choice are read only walking, and the phone keeps that as [ran at the door]. In scene 4 a runner may run again, easy, once the beats have reached the song's tempo, where a brisk walk and an easy jog meet. In scene 5 she asks a runner to walk for the knock question, then it's free.

**The logic, beyond episode 1's:**

- Shape walks for Vega. While Shape moves, they count Shape's steps instead of hers, and she can go where she isn't allowed. On the loop a stop is harmless: she is doing her round, and if she stands still too there is nothing to see for half a minute.
- From the door on she is where she isn't allowed, and nobody inside the works stands still, so she has to keep walking, and a stop she didn't ask for turns the lamp on. In this episode the lamp is only mood: the tone, the flat voice, her muffled voice. The whole branch plays only the first time; after that a stop gives only the tone and her muffled voice. No suspicion carries on into episode 3, however long Shape stands.
- Inside the works she can't ask by a stop, so yes becomes two quick knocks, close together, on the pocket where the phone is, while walking, after her Now. She says Now only when the phone reads walking. The phone reads a knock as a jolt, and that is how she sees it. Her own knock is a sound on her side, never a vibration. From this episode on, knocks are how Shape answers her.
- The mission in scene 2: the map gives the outline of the nearest water, woods, bridge or hill near Shape, and the phone measures to the nearest point of that outline, never its middle. Inside a wood or at the water's edge counts as there. Only bridges a walker can be on or under, never a railway or a motorway, and no reservoirs, basins or wastewater. She says warmer or colder until Shape is there, and asks Shape to stop. When Shape stops she loses Shape, as always, but in the last moment before, the picture holds still, and that is when she sees it. She finds that out the first time she tries it. If the map has nothing near Shape, she doesn't pretend: Shape picks a place, and she sees the sky. If the bell comes first, she goes anyway.
- Fast, once: in a stretch of the works that is lit all the way, she needs more cover than a walk gives. Faster means faster than Shape's own steps just before, still walking, read on the step rate.
- Behind the presses everyone walks in place in time with the beats, because in time nobody stands out in the noise. The beats don't follow Shape: the Company pulls the tempo up in the last quarter hour of the shift. The workers speak one word per beat, because in the middle of a beat nobody hears anything. They can't hear what counts her steps, but they can hear her talk: when she talks to Shape, she talks straight out into the air.

**Reserve in every scene:** if both stand still longer than half a minute, the earpiece asks and she answers, with episode 1's lines.

**Voices:** Vega, recorded with Jessica. The earpiece, one line, as in episode 1. The sneezes and the woman humming are sounds on her side, never Vega's voice.

**Music:** The Way We Are in scene 4, as it is: the master, 135 beats a minute all the way through. It comes whole when Shape walks in time at the song's tempo, otherwise only in bits through the noise. Only in the private copy, never in the public app.

**From episode 1 (the phone remembers):** [answered] if Shape stopped at any question or request in episode 1, otherwise [didn't answer]. [suspicion] if the lamp came on at the bell, otherwise [no suspicion]. If the memory is missing: didn't answer, no suspicion.
