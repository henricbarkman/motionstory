# Regibordet: the look

The room is a directing desk since 2026-10-28 (it was a word processor, the
manuscript room). It has no look of its own. It follows Glimt's shared one,
decided 2026-10-10 for the app, the plan, the test page and the room, and
turned back to the portal's lunar punk, stripped, on 2026-10-11:

- Rules and reasons: [`../../design/DESIGN.md`](../../design/DESIGN.md), the section "The room".
- Tokens, typefaces, the name and the verdict marks: `../../design/glimt.css` (linked by `rummet/index.html`).

What the desk adds, in `rummet/rummet.css`:

- One column, a play button in a narrow column to the left of each line. Her
  lines in Literata at `--s2` (`--s1` on a phone), directions in italic
  `--dampad`, as the room set them. Under each line one quiet sentence in
  Atkinson `--s-1` `--svag`: the voice, and what plays (a take, the app's
  recording, or nothing yet).
- **The silence is space.** The pause before a line is drawn as room between
  the lines, taller the longer the pause, with its length and where it comes
  from (the desk, the manuscript, the app) beside a `--linje` hairline. A
  pause mark in the manuscript is not drawn as text; it is that silence.
- A line opens into a drawer under it, marked by a `--linje` hairline on the
  left, not a box: pause before and after (steppers), voice (a choice: the
  pressed one filled `--mint`), the text the voice gets with its v4 tags, the
  sound, the takes (a radio list with hairlines between rows), **Gör om**
  (the screen's one filled button) and "Skriv om repliken" (a text button).
- A rewrite's state under the line in `--orkide` (a proposal's colour: it is
  on its way into Manus), a refused one in `--varm`.
- A playing line's words turn `--mint`; nothing else moves.

`skarmar/` holds screenshots of the built desk, taken by
`scripts/test_regibord_browser.py --skarmar`.
