# Manusrummet: the look

The room has no look of its own. It follows Glimt's shared one, decided
2026-10-10 for the app, the plan, the test page and the room, and turned
back to the portal's lunar punk, stripped, on 2026-10-11:

- Rules and reasons: [`../../design/DESIGN.md`](../../design/DESIGN.md), the section "The room".
- Tokens, typefaces, the name and the verdict marks: `../../design/glimt.css` (linked by `rummet/index.html`).
- What it should look like: `../../design/referens.html`, the last section, "Manusrummet".

`rummet/rummet.css` adds only the room's layout. `skarmar/` holds screenshots
of the built room, taken by `scripts/test_rummet_browser.py --skarmar`.

The lunar punk + HELD look of 2026-10-08 (and its `skiss.html`) and the blue
hour of 2026-10-10 are gone; both are in git history if anyone needs them.
